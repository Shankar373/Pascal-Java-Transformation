# Format, one fixture per line:
#   <relative path>|<expected>|[<platform overrides>]

#                by host, e.g. a Windows-only unit.
#
# Lines beginning with '#' and blank lines are ignored.

# removing an entry is also a MISMATCH (unexpected fixture).  Both fail the
# build, so the table cannot drift away from corpus/.
